# Paid Media Audit Framework

A practical, channel-agnostic framework for reviewing paid acquisition against business objectives—not platform metrics alone.

## Purpose

This repository documents a repeatable audit process for identifying measurement gaps, inefficient spend and opportunities for better decision-making.

It is designed to be adapted across Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads and DV360.

## Audit sequence

### 1. Business alignment

- Define the commercial objective behind the investment
- Clarify the role paid media should play in the customer journey
- Separate incremental demand from demand that already existed
- Establish the business metrics that should guide decisions

### 2. Measurement integrity

- Confirm primary and secondary conversion events
- Check for duplicated, missing or misclassified events
- Review GA4, Google Tag Manager and platform consistency
- Identify attribution assumptions that may distort performance

### 3. Account and campaign structure

- Review naming, segmentation and campaign architecture
- Check whether objectives, bidding and optimization events align
- Identify unnecessary fragmentation or consolidation
- Evaluate brand and non-brand separation where relevant

### 4. Audience and targeting

- Review prospecting, retargeting and exclusion logic
- Check geographic, demographic and placement controls
- Assess audience overlap and learning limitations
- Compare targeting decisions with the actual buying context

### 5. Creative and landing-page alignment

- Map messages to audiences and funnel stages
- Review the clarity of the offer and next action
- Check message continuity between ad and landing page
- Identify meaningful creative hypotheses to test

### 6. Budget and optimization

- Review allocation by objective, channel and stage
- Identify spend that cannot be tied to a clear learning goal
- Evaluate bidding constraints and scaling assumptions
- Separate short-term efficiency from long-term growth signals

### 7. Reporting and learning agenda

- Prioritize commercially useful metrics
- Document findings, risks and open questions
- Define the next tests and their decision criteria
- Set a clear review cadence

## Decision output

Each audit should end with a concise action map:

- **Scale:** evidence supports additional investment
- **Hold:** maintain while gathering better evidence
- **Fix:** resolve measurement, structure or experience issues first
- **Pause:** stop activity that lacks a defensible role

## Status

Working framework. Checklists and examples will evolve as the methodology develops.
